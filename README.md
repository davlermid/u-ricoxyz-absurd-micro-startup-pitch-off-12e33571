## Entry

> An agent that names other people's houseplants for $1 each, because plant parents can't decide and the agent refuses to do anything harder.

houseplants), the buyer is plausible (plant parents who can't decide), the
revenue is plausible ($1 per name), and the agent's laziness is the product
itself — it refuses to do anything harder.
